How the United States route works

Source verified

We work with licensed exporters in United States. Their trading documents and inspection partners are checked before a vehicle is quoted.

Inspected before payment

An independent inspection is arranged at the supplier or auction house, with photographs and a condition report sent to you.

Export and freight

Export documentation is prepared, the vehicle is moved to the export port and loaded, with marine insurance from the moment it leaves the yard.

Clearance and delivery

Our clearing partners handle duty, excise, VAT and port charges at destination, then arrange delivery to your city.

What to check before you commit

Confirm the vehicle's age against your country's import limit, confirm the drive side, and ask for the chassis number on the invoice to match the inspection report. Duties are calculated on the CIF value, so freight and insurance change the tax you pay. Our calculator shows every line before you commit, and every figure is marked as an estimate until a licensed agent confirms it.
