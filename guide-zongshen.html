Importing Zongshen motorcycles in bulk

Zongshen motorcycles are popular with boda operators, dealers and cooperatives because parts are available and the price per unit is low. Bulk importing changes a few things compared with importing a car.

Crate packing and volume

Motorcycles ship crated, usually two to four units per crate depending on model. Volume, not weight, drives your freight cost, which is why a consolidated crate of mixed models is often cheaper per bike than a single unit.

Serial numbers must match

Every frame and engine number on the commercial invoice has to match the crates and the bill of lading. Mismatches are the most common reason shipments get stuck at destination. We record serials against your order before the crate is sealed.

Duty treatment

Motorcycles are classified separately from cars, with their own duty and excise treatment by engine size. The calculator applies the destination rule for the cc of the model you are importing, so a 150 cc commuter and a 400 cc adventure bike are not assumed to cost the same.

Spare parts in the same shipment

Shipping a parts allowance with the bikes is far cheaper than a separate consignment later. Tell us which consumables you want in the crate: chains, sprockets, brake pads, cables and filters are the usual list.

This guide is general information, not customs or legal advice. Rules change and are applied case by case.
