Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay the full vehicle price upfront?

No. A booking deposit secures the vehicle, then agreed milestone payments follow: on purchase, on shipment, and the balance before delivery. Exact terms are in your quotation.

How are duties calculated?

Each destination country has its own rule set in our system: duty, excise by engine size, VAT and clearance charges. The calculator applies the destination's rules to the CIF value and shows every line. Figures are estimates until a licensed clearing agent confirms them.

Can I inspect the vehicle before it ships?

Yes. We arrange an independent third-party inspection and send the report with photographs and video before the purchase stage is authorised.

What if the vehicle is not eligible in my country?

We check age limits, drive side and emissions rules before quotation. If eligibility is uncertain we tell you and pause rather than take payment.

Are the vehicles on this website real supplier stock?

Every vehicle shown is clearly labelled demonstration data. Once supplier onboarding is complete, live inventory will replace these listings and will carry the supplier's own photographs and inspection reports.
